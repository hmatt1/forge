## Implementation Notes

**For `declareBlockersFor`**: This is your biggest win. Control decks live or die by blocking decisions. The LLM can evaluate:
- Life total pressure
- Trading creature value
- Preserving key threats
- Setting up favorable trades

**For `getCardsToDiscard`**: Control decks need sophisticated discard logic - what to keep for answers vs. what to pitch.

**For `chooseNumber`**: X-spells and variable effects are common in control - the LLM can optimize these based on game state.

**Consider Enhanced Context**: For these overrides, you may want to pass additional context to the LLM beyond what's in the base `GameStateDto`, such as:
- Opponent's likely next plays
- Current win/loss probability
- Resource constraints over next few turns

The `declareBlockersFor` override alone would likely have changed the game outcome significantly, as AI1 took 19+ damage from unblocked creatures.
